California courts

Civil filings in California superior courts

Real statewide data for unlimited civil cases, which are claims over $25,000. Most of our litigation falls in this category.

+48%

Growth in unlimited civil filings from FY 2015–16 (201,721) to FY 2024–25 (299,534).

2.6×

Rise in non-vehicle injury, property damage, and wrongful death filings in three years, from 22,495 (FY 2021–22) to 59,077 (FY 2024–25).

65%

Share of general unlimited civil cases resolved within 12 months in FY 2024–25, up from a low of 60% in FY 2022–23 but below 71% in FY 2019–20.

Source: Judicial Council of California, 2026 Court Statistics Report: Statewide Caseload Trends 2015–16 Through 2024–25, Appendix F (data for figures 3–22). Small claims appeals (2,207 filings in FY 2024–25) are left out of the case-type chart but included in totals.
