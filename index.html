Common questions

Before you call.

Does the first consultation cost anything?

No. The first 30-minute consultation is free, by phone, video, or at our Market Street office.

How do your fees work?

Personal injury cases are handled on contingency, so you pay no attorney fee unless we win. Business, real estate, and estate planning work is billed hourly or at a flat fee that we agree on in writing before work begins.

What should I bring to my consultation?

Any contracts, letters, photos, medical records, insurance information, or court papers related to your matter, plus a short timeline of what happened.

Is there a deadline to file my case?

Usually, yes. In California, many personal injury claims must be filed within two years, and claims against a city or public agency often require a written claim within six months. Contact us early so a deadline does not pass.
